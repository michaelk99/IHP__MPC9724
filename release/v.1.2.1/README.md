# sg13cmos5l_dsa1_dig_top v1.2.1

The DSA1 chip for the IHP CMOS5L MPW run of 2026-11-09, 1.3 x 1.3 mm in a QFN24: the digital back end `dsa1_dig` and dsm2, the first-order delta-sigma modulator in its oscillator variant of 2026-11-02, from branch `dsm2/integration` of this repository. The release is commit e23d046 (2026-11-04), which wrote the GDS. Later commits, most after IHP's audit, change the documents, the READMEs and `packaging/result.md`, add `netlist/lvs_reference/` and remove the stale schematic CDL. None changes the GDS.

It differs from v1.2.0 in the chip glue only: dsm2 runs from power-up with no SPI write. After `rst_n` it is enabled, leaves reset 9 to 10 `clk` periods (137 to 153 us) later, and `do_q` and `do_q_b` follow its bit stream, so it can be probed even if the SPI fails. `DSM_EN`, `DSM_RST_B` and `DSM_QOUT_EN` keep their names and reset 0 and are inverted at chip level, so writing 1 turns each off. `dsa1_dig`, dsm2, `decap_vdda` and the floorplan are those of v1.2.0. The new cells cost 6 `DECAPHV32`, VDD has 82.21 pF.

Board rules that matter on first power-up: a pull-down on `rst_n`, held low until the supplies are up, VDDA no later than VDD and both no later than IOVDD (the start-up bench's `core_first`), supplies up before `vi` is driven, no DC load on `vcm`, `dsm_clk` pulled low through a resistor and driven through a series resistor, the host lets go of `dsm_clk` before it sets `di_clk_sel` (`BIAS_CFG_0` bit 8), and about 40 decimator words are discarded after dsm2 leaves reset. Program 1 of the bench host stops at its `CHIP_ID` check on this die, program 8 replaces it.

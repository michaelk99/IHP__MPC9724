Release notes:

v1.1-openblau:
       lef/ez130_tech.lef retargeted from the SG13G2 metal stack to the
       SG13CMOS5L one (Metal1 to Metal4 plus TopMetal1). No device or
       pin changed. See doc/sg13cmos5l_adaptation.md.
       gds/ez130_8t.gds: each cell's 235/4 boundary copied onto 189/4
       (prBoundary.boundary) by scripts/add_prboundary.py.

v1.1:  First public release of the library after optimizations performed
       by the lecturers. The library contains 220 cells.
v1.0:  Version compiled after all VLSI 5 students submitted their cells,
       for a total of 216 cells.
v0:    Initial version of the library with 39 cells, used as a starting 
       point for the spring'25 ``VLSI 5'' course at ETH Zurich.

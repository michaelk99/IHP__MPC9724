# Supply start-up bench

The chip as the measurement bench meets it: the supplies ramping, the host holding `rst_n` low through the ramp, then SPI reads through the pads. Our tapeout sign-off requires this simulation.

```
testbenches/startup/run_startup.sh                       inside IIC-OSIC-TOOLS, at the repo root, the AGENTS.md environment set
STARTUP_DSM2=pex testbenches/startup/run_startup.sh      dsm2's extracted netlist instead of its schematic, STARTUP_DSM2=box for the old bench sources
testbenches/startup/run_dsm2_mutant.sh                   core_first with dsm2's vdd on VDD, which must fail
testbenches/startup/run_power_v111.sh <worktree> <tag>   the DSA1 state on an older tag's chip, for the report's comparison
testbenches/startup/cell_leakage.py --v111 ...          the DC current of each IO cell type, to explain the comparison
```

It writes `verification/startup/startup_report.md`, `startup.png` and `bounce.png` (`STARTUP_OUT` for another directory) and exits non-zero on a failed check. The five orders run side by side, `io_first`, `core_first`, `bounce`, `power_dsa1` and `power_dsm`.

## What it simulates

- **The ESD clamp cell** at transistor level from its CDL, `ip/sg13cmos5l_ip__clamp_vddvss`.
- **IO cells as transistors**, from the pinned image's PDK, `libs.ref/sg13cmos5l_io/spice/sg13cmos5l_io.spice`, the PDK the layout's IO cell GDS comes from, for every tree including a v1.1.1 worktree. The repo's snapshot `ip/sg13cmos5l_io_custom` is another PDK version with other LevelDown and clamp diode devices and is not simulated. Models are the typical HV and LV MOS, `rppd` and diode models. `sub!`, the body the IO cells' `rppd` resistors take, is tied to ground. The two clamp copies are in, the cut cells are empty subcircuits, the filler cells carry nothing and the rails of the VDD and VDDA bands are ideal nets.
- **Three core supplies**: IOVDD at 3.3 V, VDD at 1.2 V and VDDA at 1.5 V. The pads of the VDDA segment are the three analog pads, `dsm_clk`, `do_q`, `do_q_b` and the VDDA supply pad.
- **dsm2 as transistors** in `io_first`, `core_first` and `bounce`: its schematic netlist from `ip/dsm2_core/spice` by default, its extracted one with `STARTUP_DSM2=pex`, never edited, included inside a wrapper subcircuit (`build/dsm2.spice`) so that its 5-pin `sg13cmos5l_SecondaryProtection` stays apart from the IO cells' 4-pin one. Its supply is VDDA and its vss VSS as in the chip netlist, with a 0 V source in its vdd that measures its own current. Its 13 control inputs come from the core through the same scaled sources as the pad outputs, `do_q` and `do_q_b` drive their pads, `do_q_dec` goes into the core's A-to-D bridge with 15 fF, the DEC_SRC gate as dsm2's bench loads it. Corners are typical, as for the rest of the bench, with `cornerCAP.lib` for its MoM caps.
- **dsm2 as a box** in the power orders, which have no frames to start it, and in every order with `STARTUP_DSM2=box`. The `do_q` and `do_q_b` pads then get bench sources on the VDDA regulator side with 1 kOhm, which toggle at every rising `dsm_clk` edge, complementary, 5 ns after it, in `power_dsm` (and `bounce` with the box) and sit low otherwise, and `do_q_dec` is held low through 1 GOhm.
- **The adc_conv gate** is left out of the core and its pad is driven the same way on the VDD side, with one pulse of one clk cycle in `bounce`, because the real pulse comes once per output sample, 1.95 ms apart. The pad inputs driven this way are found from the netlist, the ones no cell drives.
- **The core as XSPICE.** `gen_startup.py` flattens the macro's powered netlist into the chip's and keeps every functional cell, 11666 of 72 types. `spi2xspice.py` turns them into XSPICE gates and flops from the EZ130 typical liberty. Two fixes come first: the liberty is cut to the used cells, because the flop banks' bundles stop its parser, and every pin is renamed `P0Q`, `P1Q`, ..., because it substitutes pin names as text and `B` corrupts `AB`. The 26 `CGX3` clock gates on the sclk tree are a `statetable` it cannot read, so `--patch-xspice` models each as a latch open while CK is low and an AND. Alone, with ideal inputs, this core reads all three registers right.
- **Core outputs follow the core supply.** XSPICE drives a fixed 1.2 V, so each output into a pad goes through a source scaled by the regulator side of VDD and 1 kOhm, a standard cell's output.
- **The host on the IOVDD rail**: every pin it drives, `dsm_clk` among them, is scaled by the regulator side of IOVDD and goes through 50 Ohm. Its clocks, clk and sclk, start 20 us before it releases `rst_n`, after both ramps. mosi moves on sclk's rising edges, because the slave samples on the falling ones, as the host's I2S engine does.
- **Supplies with 0.5 Ohm** of output resistance, grounds with 0.1 Ohm.
- **The orders.** `core_first` is the board order, VDD and VDDA from 10 us and IOVDD from 150 us, each a 100 us ramp. `io_first` is the reverse, IOVDD first. `rst_n` is released at 300 us, then CHIP_ID, TEST_CTRL_0 and AUX_CTRL_0 are read at 400 kHz. With dsm2 as transistors the host then starts it as `doc/specifications.md` says: BIAS_CFG_1 = 0x0041 (DSM_EN, ADC_CONV_EN kept) takes effect at 587.5 us, 0x0063 (DSM_RST_B and DSM_QOUT_EN too) 130 us later at 717.5 us, and the run ends at 760 us. `dsm_clk` runs at 1.024 MHz from 5 us before DSM_RST_B, 43 periods, since each costs minutes with dsm2 in. dsm2 waits enabled and unclocked. `vi` is driven at half of the VDDA regulator voltage through 50 Ohm and `vcm` is left to dsm2's vcm_gen. With dsm2 a box `vi` and `vcm` are open (1 MOhm to ground). `ibias_test` is open in every order.
- **Ground bounce (`bounce`)**: VDD and VDDA first, and the package between die and board. Each bond wire is 1 nH and 50 mOhm per mm of its length in `packaging/result.md`, plus 0.5 nH and 10 mOhm of QFN lead. VSS and IOVSS downbond to the paddle, which the leads to the cavity and the exposed pad (0.1 nH) tie to the board ground, as bonded, with every downbond of a net in parallel. 100 nF at each supply pin, 10 pF on each output pin. With dsm2 as transistors the supplies are up from the start, without the ramps: dsm2's VDDA ramp needs backward Euler (below), which the package's wires slowed to 0.2 us of simulated time per minute. CHIP_ID is read at 320 us, DSM_EN takes effect at 442.5 us, SPARE_14 (16 free bits) is written with 0x5555 in the wait, DSM_RST_B with DSM_QOUT_EN at 572.5 us, `dsm_clk` runs from 567.5 us, and SPARE_14 is read back at 580 us with dsm2 running, which toggles `miso` at every sclk bit, with one `adc_conv` pulse inside that read at 586 us. The run ends at 650 us. `vi` comes from a source at half of the VDDA regulator voltage through 50 Ohm and `vcm` from vcm_gen. With dsm2 a box the frames start at 320 us, `dsm_clk` and the toggling `do_q` sources at 290 us, and `vi` and `vcm` both come from the half-VDDA source.
- **The DSA1 power state (`power_dsa1`, `power_dsm`)**: no SPI frames, `sclk` idle low, `clk` and `data_in` toggling at 65.536 kHz, `data_in` on clk's falling edges. `power_dsa1` is dsm2 off with `dsm_clk` low. `power_dsm` has `dsm_clk` at 1.024 MHz and the `do_q` pads switching, with DEC_SRC low. The currents are the mean over the last 8 data_in periods, 125 dsm_clk periods, so the toggling averages out.

## Solver settings, and why

The IO cells' clamps, level shifters and antenna diodes stall ngspice ("timestep too small") or make it creep without end while one supply is near 0 or at the edges. What it took, each found by a run that stalled without it:
- the largest step capped at 200 ns, since the default, a fiftieth of the run, strides into the clamps' turn-on
- the antenna diodes with a transit time of 0 (`diodes_bench.lib`, a copy of the PDK's `diodes.lib`): their 700 ns stalled the solver when ground bounce nudged one forward, and here they only ever sit reverse biased or near 0
- the host's and the core's sources scaled by the regulator sides, not by the chip's own rails, which formed a loop with the pads
- no clock edge during a ramp: one into a pad on a rail of a few 10 mV stalled the solver for good
- with dsm2 as transistors, breakpoint sources every 0.25 ns from 1 to 6 ns after both `dsm_clk` edges, as dsm2's loop benches have them from 1 to 4 ns after di_clk, plus 2 ns for the pad. Without them dsm2 alone stalls about 16 clocks after `di_rst_b` at its vi ESD diode, with its own header and bias too
- with dsm2 as transistors, `.options rshunt=1e12` in `build/dsm2.spice` under every recipe, from dsm2's own header, its operating point needs it. In `io_first` and `core_first` also `.options maxord=1` in the stimulus, which turns gear into backward Euler: with the second order dsm2 alone stalls on a VDDA ramp near 0.35 V, at `vcm` and its switched-cap nodes, with every recipe below and with dsm2's own tolerances, and in the chip the stall shows up at an IO cell's diode. It costs about three times the steps of the second order, and it holds for the whole run, since ngspice keeps the order it started with when an `option` is set between `stop` and `resume`
- `run_startup.sh`'s `RECIPES`, tried in turn: 1 fF on every node, then a looser reltol, then 1 TOhm to ground, then the trapezoidal method, with a watchdog that ends an attempt after 15 minutes without progress. Each order's report names the recipe that ran

## What it checks

Per supply order, in `startup_check.py`:
- the three reads return 0x0001, 0x0500 and 0xFF00
- no supply current (IOVDD, VDD, VDDA) stays above 10 mA for more than 1 us, as latch-up or fighting drivers would. Shorter peaks are reported
- in the board order the `do_q` and `do_q_b` pads stay below 0.33 V. For IOVDD first the pads' rise is reported, the spike the board rule avoids

With dsm2 as transistors, in the start-up orders and `bounce`:
- the controls reach dsm2 when the writes take effect, di_ena at DSM_EN, di_rst_b and di_qout_en 130 us later, each within 1 us
- dsm2's own VDDA current within its design values: the mean from 10 us after DSM_RST_B to the end at most 28 uA, the largest mean over 100 ns at most 140 uA (dsm2 peer, 2026-10-31, schematic ff 125 C, quantizer fight excluded) and over 10 ns at most 600 uA, both from DSM_EN on. dsm2 measured its enable inrush at ff 1.55 V 125 C as 137 uA over 100 ns and 365 uA over 10 ns, 64 and 240 uA at tt, and gives 370 uA over 10 ns as its worst case, which the report quotes. The current before DSM_EN and in reset are reported. Above 100 uA there the run is in dsm2 6dd2926's known latch fight (its quantizer output latch holding a 1 against its preset, about 200 uA, fixed GDS due 2026-11-02): the report names it with its numbers and judges the peaks only from the end of the fight after DSM_RST_B
- the modulator runs: one sample per dsm_clk period from 10 us after DSM_RST_B, the `do_q` pad 30 to 70 % ones with vi at half VDDA, `do_q_b` its complement and `do_q_dec` equal to `do_q` in at least 95 % of the periods
- in the start-up orders, the VDD current with dsm2 running differs from the one before DSM_EN, both in stretches without frames, by at most 10 % of dsm2's current. dsm2's vdd on VDD fails here (`run_dsm2_mutant.sh`)
- the do_q pads' quiet check ends at DSM_QOUT_EN. The vi and ibias_test pad currents and, with IOVDD first, the VDDA source current while VDDA is down are reported

For `bounce`: the CHIP_ID and SPARE_14 reads, that the stimulus ran (dsm_clk at 90 % of its edges, do_q and do_q_b at 90 % with the box and at least one edge per four periods from dsm2, at least 12 miso edges in the SPARE_14 read, the adc_conv pulse), and no core input crossing its threshold more often than the host's pin does between 295 us and the end. It reports the die's VSS, IOVSS and paddle against the board ground, droop and overshoot of IOVDD to IOVSS, VDD to VSS and VDDA to VSS on the die, the peak current in the supply wires, and the swing of `vi`, `vcm` and `ibias_test` on the die (the `padres` side of the analog pads) against their quiet level.

For the power orders: the VDD current of `power_dsm` must equal the one of `power_dsa1` within the resolution of the runs, the larger of the difference between the window's two halves and 0.1 % of the current. With `verification/startup/power_v111.json` present, the report also compares the DSA1 state with the v1.1.1 chip.

`run_isolation_mutant.sh` is a real broken input for the isolation check: a copy of the netlist with the `vdd` pins of the two `do_q` pads on VDD must make the check fail, and it requires that. It runs `power_dsm` on the copy.

Each check has a broken input in the self-tests (`--self-test` of both scripts): a sustained current, one on the VDDA wire, a wrong read, a pad spike, a glitch, a 1 % rise of VDD with dsm2 running, a dead aggressor in the bounce, and traces or currents missing from the data, which fail instead of passing.

## What it cannot say

- **The power-up state of the flops.** XSPICE starts every flop at 0. Silicon starts them anywhere, and the chip has no power-on reset, so until the first `rst_n` low the registers, and with them the dsm2 control inputs, are undefined. The host must hold `rst_n` low until all supplies are up, which this bench assumes and does not prove.
- **The core's supply current.** The XSPICE gates draw nothing from VDD, so the VDD current is the IO cells' and the clamps' only. The core's own is 3.061 uW typical on v1.1.1, from `make power-vcd`.
- **dsm2 in the power orders.** They keep the box, so the VDD isolation check there sees the do_q pads switching from bench sources, not dsm2's own current. The start-up orders check VDD with dsm2 running instead.
- **dsm2's power-up.** Before DSM_EN dsm2's logic sees VDDA ramping from 0 with its controls low, which the bench simulates with backward Euler. Its bit-level accuracy in the loop is dsm2's own benches' subject, not this one's.
- Package, bond wire and board parasitics in the two supply orders, and any corner but typical. The bounce case has the package, with assumed wire and board values. Its core draws no current and its decap cells are not in it, so VDD to VSS there shows the IO cells' core side only.
